**Event:** [FOSS4G 2026 Hiroshima](https://2026.foss4g.org), Hiroshima, Japan

This lightning talk, co-presented with Alison Lenaerts, introduced the **MAPME Initiative** (Monitoring, Assessment, and Planning for Measurable Effectiveness), a community-driven initiative promoting access to open GIS resources for planning, monitoring and evaluation in international development.

The initiative is built on four pillars:

- **Community:** a collaborative community sharing knowledge on geospatial methods for development
- **Open tools:** open analytical tools for monitoring and impact evaluation, such as the [mapme.biodiversity](https://github.com/mapme-initiative/mapme.biodiversity) package
- **Projects:** real-world collaborative projects applying geospatial methods
- **Geo4Impact:** an event promoting innovation around open GIS for sustainable development

The talk showcased the open-source tools, demonstrated how geospatial data drives development impact, and explained how participants can contribute.
