RAPIDA assesses the exposure of population, buildings, roads, electrical grid, land use and socioeconomic indicators over an area of interest using curated global datasets, and publishes the results to UNDP GeoHub.

**Tech stack:** Python, GDAL, rasterio, GeoPandas, Click, Azure Blob Storage, JupyterHub
