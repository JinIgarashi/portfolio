Adds a control that exports the current map as PDF, PNG, JPEG or SVG with selectable paper size, orientation and DPI. Originally forked from mapbox-gl-export and widely used in the MapLibre community.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, MapLibre GL JS, jsPDF
