Adds a legend control that builds a legend panel automatically from the MapLibre style, using symbols rendered by [legend-symbol](https://github.com/orangemug/legend-symbol). Originally forked from mapbox-gl-legend.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, MapLibre GL JS
