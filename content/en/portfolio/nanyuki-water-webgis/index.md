A WebGIS for Nanyuki Water and Sewerage Co., Ltd. in Kenya, built by applying the vector tile approach developed for Narok Water. It shows pipelines with their diameter and material, tanks and other water facilities over satellite imagery, and provides measuring, printing and elevation tools.

**Tech stack:** PostgreSQL/PostGIS, vector tiles, MapLibre GL JS, SvelteKit
