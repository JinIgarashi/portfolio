GeoHub is a centralized ecosystem of geospatial data and services at UNDP. Users without geospatial skills can search, visualize and download datasets, upload their own data, run raster analytics and share maps. I developed many of its core features as a Full Stack GIS Developer, including the data upload pipeline, authentication, permission management and map styling UI.

**Tech stack:** SvelteKit, MapLibre GL JS, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, titiler, pg_tileserv, STAC, Azure
