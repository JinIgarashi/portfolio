Converts GeoJSON layers of junctions, pipes, pumps, reservoirs, tanks and valves into an EPANET INP file, making it easy to bridge GIS databases and hydraulic modelling. Published on npm as `@watergis/geojson2inp`.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, Node.js, EPANET
