## Overview

FOSS4G (Free and Open Source Software for Geospatial) is the global annual conference of the Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo). The 2026 edition was held in Hiroshima, Japan. I served as a volunteer member of the Local Organizing Committee (LOC) to prepare and run the conference.

## Roles

- **PR / Communication:** promoted the conference and communicated with the community
- **Participant / Delegate:** supported conference participants and delegates
