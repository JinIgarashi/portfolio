**Period:** November 2018 – November 2019

**Client:** The Government of Thailand

**Funded by:** Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA)

**Role:** Consultant, Kokusai Kogyo Co., Ltd.

## Overview

GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite System) positioning with CORS (Continuously Operating Reference Stations) enables highly accurate positioning, which is the basis of applications such as surveying, precision agriculture and autonomous driving. This survey collected information for establishing an experimental field for GNSS system development in Thailand, including a CORS data centre.

## My contribution

- Surveyed and organized information on the establishment of the CORS data centre.
