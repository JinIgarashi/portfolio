**Period:** 2021 – 2022 (MSc dissertation)

**Organization:** School of Civil Engineering, University of Leeds, United Kingdom

**Project:** CACTUS (Climate and Costs in Urban Sanitation), funded by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation

## Overview

For my MSc (Eng) in Water, Sanitation and Health Engineering (passed with distinction), I researched the full lifecycle costs of urban sanitation systems under the CACTUS project. A case study of Narok town, Kenya was conducted to estimate the cost of the urban sanitation system over the whole sanitation value chain — containment, emptying, transport, treatment and end-use/disposal.

The study built on my long-term partnership with Narok Water and Sewerage Services Co., Ltd., where I previously worked as a [GIS specialist](../201404narok-water/).

## Outputs

- [Shit-flow diagram (SFD) Lite report in Narok town, Kenya](../../publication/naroktown-sfd/) — published at sfd.susana.org, showing the latest sanitation situation in the municipality (October 2022)
